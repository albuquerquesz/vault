# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who want to organize their personal finances without leaving a technical, tool-first environment. They want a clear place to connect accounts, review transactions, understand monthly cash flow, and keep spending decisions grounded in real numbers instead of rough intuition.

## Product Purpose

Vault helps developers understand and manage their personal finances through a focused dashboard experience. It exists to reduce friction around tracking income, expenses, recurring bills, goals, and connected bank data, so users can make calmer monthly decisions with less manual effort.

## Brand Personality

Precise, minimalist, confident. The product should feel technically fluent, quiet, and deliberate. It should communicate competence without showing off, with the emotional goal of replacing financial noise with clarity and control.

## Anti-references

Avoid crypto-style neon, glow-heavy, hype-driven interfaces. Avoid generic blue corporate banking dashboards that feel bureaucratic or impersonal. Avoid bloated SaaS dashboards filled with decorative cards, fake metrics, and visual noise that makes the product feel templated.

## Design Principles

- Make financial clarity feel native to technical users.
- Prefer signal over spectacle.
- Keep the interface calm under frequent use.
- Let structure, spacing, and typography do the heavy lifting.
- Treat every number as operational data, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target solid everyday accessibility for a data-dense product UI, with clear contrast, legible hierarchy, keyboard-friendly interactions, and restrained motion. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate state, and preserve readability for long dashboard sessions in both dark and light themes.
